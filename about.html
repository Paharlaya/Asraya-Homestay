About

A house that keeps
its own quiet

The house

Above the tea estate,
below the park

Asraya is a charming homestay nestled away from the crowded part of town, perfectly situated above the Happy Valley Tea Estate and below Shrubbery Nightingale Park.

Our beautiful, cozy rooms offer panoramic views of the valley, hills, and the picturesque tea gardens surrounding Darjeeling. Enjoy a serene and tranquil stay with us, surrounded by nature’s beauty.

Each of our rooms is tastefully decorated, blending modern amenities with the warmth of traditional Darjeeling hospitality. Enjoy plush bedding, elegant furnishings, and picturesque views that will leave you mesmerized.

At the table

Vegetarian, home-cooked,
from the market that morning

Meals are prepared in the house from locally sourced, fresh ingredients, with an emphasis on hygiene and the authentic culinary traditions of the region.

Queen of the Hills

Four rooms,
four namesakes

Darjeeling, known as the ‘Queen of the Hills,’ is famous for its breathtaking landscapes and vibrant culture. With a rich tradition of music and a legacy of Hollywood actors originating from this charming town, Darjeeling exudes an artistic spirit.

Inspired by this cultural heritage, ‘Asraya’ homestay embraces a Hollywood theme, offering a unique and memorable experience amidst the beauty of the hills. Each of the four rooms carries the name of a performer with roots in these hills.
