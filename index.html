Asraya is a charming homestay nestled away from the crowded part of town, perfectly situated above the Happy Valley Tea Estate and below Shrubbery Nightingale Park.

Our beautiful, cozy rooms offer panoramic views of the valley, hills, and the picturesque tea gardens surrounding Darjeeling. Enjoy a serene and tranquil stay with us, surrounded by nature’s beauty.

Queen of the Hills

Why the rooms
carry these names

Darjeeling, known as the ‘Queen of the Hills,’ is famous for its breathtaking landscapes and vibrant culture. With a rich tradition of music and a legacy of Hollywood actors originating from this charming town, Darjeeling exudes an artistic spirit.

Inspired by this cultural heritage, ‘Asraya’ homestay embraces a Hollywood theme, offering a unique and memorable experience amidst the beauty of the hills.
